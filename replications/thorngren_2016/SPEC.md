## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Thorngren et al. (2016) analyze a sample of 47 cool giant exoplanets ($F_{\text{inc}} < 2 \times 10^8\,\text{erg s}^{-1}\text{ cm}^{-2}$) to determine heavy-element core masses $M_z$.

### 1. Heavy-Element Core Mass Scaling
$$M_z = 15.0 \times \left(\frac{M_p}{M_{\text{Jup}}}\right)^{0.63} \times 10^{0.51 [\text{Fe/H}]} \, M_\oplus$$

### 2. Heavy-Element Mass Fraction $Z_p$
$$Z_p = \frac{M_z}{M_p} = 0.047 \times \left(\frac{M_p}{M_{\text{Jup}}}\right)^{-0.37} \times 10^{0.51 [\text{Fe/H}]}$$
