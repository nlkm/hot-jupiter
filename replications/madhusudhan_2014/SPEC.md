# Replication Specification: Madhusudhan et al. (2014)
**Title**: H2O Abundances and C/O Ratios in Hot Jupiter Atmospheres  
**Authors**: Nikku Madhusudhan, Hannah R. Wakeford, et al.  
**Journal**: ApJ Letters, 791, L9 (2014) | **arXiv**: `1407.6054`

---

## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Madhusudhan et al. (2014) constrain water abundances and carbon-to-oxygen ratios in three benchmark hot Jupiters (HD 209458b, HD 189733b, WASP-12b), discovering systematically sub-solar $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ mixing ratios.

### 1. Carbon-to-Oxygen Ratio
$$\text{C/O} = \frac{X_{\text{CO}} + X_{\text{CH}_4}}{X_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} + X_{\text{CO}} + 2 X_{\text{CO}_2}}$$
