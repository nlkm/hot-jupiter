\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Madhusudhan et al. (2014) (\textit{ApJ Letters}, 791, L9) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Madhusudhan2014CoRatioModel}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 \ge 0.9989$) across water mixing ratio posteriors and carbon-to-oxygen (C/O) ratio distributions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Madhusudhan et al. (2014) constrain water abundances and carbon-to-oxygen ratios in benchmark hot Jupiters:
\begin{equation}
\text{C/O} = \frac{X_{\text{CO}} + X_{\text{CH}_4}}{X_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} + X_{\text{CO}} + 2 X_{\text{CO}_2}}
\end{equation}
