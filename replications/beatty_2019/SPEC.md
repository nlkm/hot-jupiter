## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Beatty et al. (2019) present Spitzer phase curve observations of the brown dwarf KELT-1b, measuring day-night heat redistribution trends across highly irradiated companions.

### 1. Recirculation Efficiency & Phase Curve Formula
$$\varepsilon = \frac{8}{5} \frac{T_{\text{night}}^4}{T_{\text{day}}^4 - T_{\text{night}}^4}$$
$$\frac{F_p}{F_\star}(\phi) = F_0 + A_{\text{phase}} \cos(\phi - \Delta \phi)$$
