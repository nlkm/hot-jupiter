## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Beatty et al. (2019) model the thermal phase curve of KELT-1b, demonstrating inefficient day-to-night heat recirculation ($\varepsilon < 0.1$).

### 1. Recirculation Efficiency
$$\varepsilon = \frac{16 \sigma T_{\text{night}}^4}{f L_\star / (4\pi a^2)}$$
