\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Beatty et al. (2019) (\textit{AJ}, 158, 166) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Beatty2019Kelt1bPhaseCurveModel}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 = 1.0000$) across KELT-1b Spitzer $3.6\,\mu\text{m}$ phase curves and heat recirculation efficiency trends ($\varepsilon < 0.1$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Beatty et al. (2019) analyze thermal recirculation efficiency in massive brown dwarf companion KELT-1b:
\begin{equation}
\varepsilon = \frac{16 \sigma T_{\text{night}}^4}{f L_\star / (4\pi a^2)}
\end{equation}
