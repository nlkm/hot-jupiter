**Journal**: The Astrophysical Journal (ApJ), 753, 100 (2012) | **arXiv**: `1203.4018`

---

## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Benneke & Seager (2012) present a Bayesian framework to infer atmospheric mean molecular weight $\mu$ and cloudiness from transmission spectra.

### 1. Transmission Spectrum Scale Height Slope Formula
$$\frac{d(R_p/R_\star)^2}{d\ln \lambda} = \frac{2 R_p}{R_\star^2} \frac{k_B T}{\mu g}$$
