## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Rappaport et al. (2013) derive the 3D isothermal sonic L1 nozzle hydrodynamic mass loss equation for planets filling their Roche lobes.

### 1. 3D Isothermal L1 Nozzle Mass Loss Rate
$$\dot{M}_{\text{RLOF}} = \rho_0 c_s \frac{2 \pi c_s^2}{\Omega^2 F_1} \exp\left( -\frac{\Delta \Phi}{c_s^2} \right)$$
where $c_s = \sqrt{k_B T / \mu m_H}$ is the isothermal sound speed and $F_1$ is the L1 Roche potential curvature.
