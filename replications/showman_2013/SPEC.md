## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Showman & Kaspi (2013) formulate scaling relations for circulation regimes, equatorial jet speeds, and Rossby deformation scale on synchronous super-Earths.

### 1. Rossby Deformation Radius & Zonal Jet Velocity
$$L_D = \frac{(N H)^{1/2}}{(2 \Omega / a)^{1/2}}$$
$$U_{\text{jet}} \sim \frac{\Delta T_{\text{eq}}}{T_{\text{eq}}} \frac{g H}{\tau_{\text{rad}} \Omega}$$
