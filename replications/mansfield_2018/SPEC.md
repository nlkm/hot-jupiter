## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Mansfield et al. (2018) present HST WFC3 secondary eclipse observations of ultra-hot Jupiter WASP-103b, revealing a water feature muted by $H_2O$ dissociation.

### 1. Thermal Emission Formula
$$\frac{F_p}{F_\star}(\lambda) = \frac{B_\lambda(T_{\text{day}})}{B_\lambda(T_\star)} \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2$$
