---

## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Mansfield et al. (2018) present high-precision HST WFC3 secondary eclipse emission spectroscopy of the ultra-hot Jupiter WASP-12b, detecting $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ emission/absorption features.

### 1. Thermal Emission Flux Ratio
$$\frac{F_p}{F_\star}(\lambda) = \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2 \frac{B_\lambda(T_{\text{day}}(\tau_\lambda=1))}{B_\lambda(T_\star)}$$
