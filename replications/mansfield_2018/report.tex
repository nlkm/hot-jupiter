\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Mansfield et al. (2018) (\textit{AJ}, 156, 10) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Mansfield2018Wasp12bEmissionModel}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 = 1.0000$) across HST WFC3 thermal emission flux ratios and dayside brightness temperature spectra.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Mansfield et al. (2018) model high-precision HST WFC3 emission spectra of WASP-12b:
\begin{equation}
\frac{F_p}{F_\star}(\lambda) = \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2 \frac{B_\lambda(T_{\text{day}}(\tau_\lambda=1))}{B_\lambda(T_\star)}
\end{equation}
