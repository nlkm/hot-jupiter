---

## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Koll & Abbot (2016) quantify how shortwave-to-longwave opacity ratio $\gamma = \kappa_{\text{SW}}/\kappa_{\text{LW}}$ and circulation timescale ratio $\tau_{\text{rad}}/\tau_{\text{adv}}$ dictate thermal inversions and day-night temperature contrasts on synchronous planets.

### 1. Day-Night Contrast & Inversion Strength
$$\Delta T_{\text{dn}} = \frac{T_{\text{eq}}}{1 + \frac{\tau_{\text{rad}}}{\tau_{\text{adv}}}}$$
$$\eta_{\text{inv}} = \frac{T_{\text{top}} - T_{\text{bottom}}}{T_{\text{eq}}}$$
