## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Carone et al. (2020) investigate the vertical extension and pressure-dependent strength of equatorial superrotation jets on WASP-43b and HD 209458b using 3D circulation models.

### 1. Vertical Zonal Wind & Pressure Dependence
$$U_{\text{eq}}(P) = U_0 \left[1 - \alpha \ln\left(\frac{P}{P_0}\right)\right]$$
