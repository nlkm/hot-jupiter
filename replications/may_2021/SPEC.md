## Executive Summary & Core Equations

May et al. (2021) analyze Spitzer $4.5\,\mu\text{m}$ phase curve observations of WASP-76b and WASP-121b, measuring eastward phase offsets and high day-night contrast driven by thermal dissociation and recirculation.

### 1. Phase Curve Flux Ratio & Thermal Emission
$$\frac{F_p}{F_\star}(\phi) = C_0 + C_1 \cos(\phi - \phi_1) + C_2 \cos(2\phi - \phi_2)$$
$$T_{\text{bright}}(\phi) = T_{\text{eff},\star} \sqrt{\frac{R_\star}{R_p}} \left[\frac{F_p}{F_\star}(\phi)\right]^{1/4}$$
