## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Welbanks et al. (2019) map mass-metallicity trends across 19 hot Jupiters, revealing sub-solar to solar water abundances despite solar system planetary trends.

### 1. Mass-Metallicity Power Law
$$[\text{M/H}] = \alpha \left(\frac{M_p}{M_{\text{Jup}}}\right)^\beta$$
$$\log_{10} X_{\text{H2O}}(M_p) = \log_{10} X_{\text{solar}} + \beta \log_{10}\left(\frac{M_p}{M_{\text{Jup}}}\right)$$
