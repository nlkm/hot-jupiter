## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Welbanks et al. (2019) analyze transmission spectra of 19 exoplanets (ranging from Super-Earths to Jupiter-mass giants), discovering a universal depletion of water $[H_2O/H]$ relative to sodium $[Na/H]$.

### 1. Mass-Metallicity Power-Law Formula
$$\log_{10} [X/H] = \alpha \log_{10}(M_p / M_\oplus) + \beta$$
