\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Welbanks et al. (2019) (\textit{ApJL}, 887, L20) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Welbanks2019MassMetallicityModel}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 \ge 0.9995$) across atmospheric water abundance scaling $\log_{10} X_{\text{H2O}}(M_p)$ and mass-metallicity trends across 19 hot Jupiters.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Welbanks et al. (2019) construct a mass-metallicity scaling relation across transiting exoplanets:
\begin{equation}
[\text{M/H}] = \alpha \left(\frac{M_p}{M_{\text{Jup}}}\right)^\beta
\end{equation}
