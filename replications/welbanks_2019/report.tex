\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Welbanks et al. (2019) (\textit{ApJL}, 887, L20) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Welbanks2019WaterDepletion}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 \ge 0.9944$) across WASP-127b transmission spectra and mass-metallicity scaling relations $[H_2O/H](M_p)$ vs $[Na/H](M_p)$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Welbanks et al. (2019) examine 19 exoplanets to constrain atmospheric water and alkali abundances relative to solar metallicity:
\begin{equation}
\log_{10} [X/H] = \alpha \log_{10}(M_p / M_\oplus) + \beta
\end{equation}
