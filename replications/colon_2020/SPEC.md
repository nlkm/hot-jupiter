## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Colón et al. (2020) present ground-based optical transmission spectroscopy of WASP-52b, detecting broad Na I and K I atomic alkali absorption.

### 1. Transmission Spectrum
$$\left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2(\lambda) = \left(\frac{R_0}{R_\star}\right)^2 + \frac{2 R_0 H}{R_\star^2} \ln \left[\frac{\sigma_{\text{Na,K}}(\lambda) P_0}{\mu g \tau_0}\right]$$
