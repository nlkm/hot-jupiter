## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Espinoza et al. (2019) report Magellan/IMACS optical transmission spectroscopy of the ultra-short-period hot Jupiter WASP-19b ($P_{\text{orb}} = 0.789\text{ days}$), confirming a clear atmosphere with strong Na I line absorption ($0.589\,\mu\text{m}$).

### 1. Optical Transmission Scale Height Formula
$$\Delta \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2 = \frac{2 R_p H}{R_\star^2} \ln\left(\frac{\sigma(\lambda)}{\sigma_0}\right)$$
