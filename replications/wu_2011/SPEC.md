## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Wu & Lithwick (2011) demonstrate that secular chaos in multi-planet systems can pump inner planet eccentricities to $e > 0.98$, triggering rapid tidal circularization into Hot Jupiter orbits.

### 1. High-Eccentricity Octupole Secular Evolution
$$\frac{de_1}{dt} = \frac{15}{4} \frac{m_2}{M_\star} n_1 \left(\frac{a_1}{a_2}\right)^3 e_1 \sqrt{1 - e_1^2} \sin(2 \omega_1)$$

### 2. Angular Momentum Conservation for Tidal Circularization
$$a_f = a_i (1 - e_i^2)$$
