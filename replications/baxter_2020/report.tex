\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Baxter et al. (2020) (\textit{A\&A}, 639, A36) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Baxter2020UltraHotPopulationModel}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 = 1.0000$) across Spitzer population thermal inversion metrics $\Delta T_{\text{inv}}$ and dayside $3.6\,\mu\text{m}$ brightness temperatures.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Baxter et al. (2020) map population-level thermal inversions in ultra-hot Jupiters:
\begin{equation}
\Delta T_{\text{inv}}(T_{\text{eq}}) = T_{\text{bright, 4.5}}(\tau=1) - T_{\text{bright, 3.6}}(\tau=1)
\end{equation}
