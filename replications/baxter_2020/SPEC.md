**Authors**: E. K. H. Baxter, V. Parmentier, et al.  
**Journal**: A&A, 639, A36 (2020) | **arXiv**: `2004.14389`

---

## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Baxter et al. (2020) model a population of 9 ultra-hot Jupiters using Spitzer secondary eclipses to show that $H^-$ opacity and thermal inversions universalize upper-atmosphere thermal structures.

### 1. Brightness Temperature & Eclipse Formula
$$\delta_{\text{ecl}}(\lambda) = \frac{B_\lambda(T_{\text{day}})}{B_\lambda(T_\star)} \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2$$
