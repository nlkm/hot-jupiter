## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Baxter et al. (2020) perform a comprehensive Spitzer population study of ultra-hot Jupiters, identifying the $T_{\text{eq}} \approx 2200\,\text{K}$ thermal inversion and $\text{H}^-$ opacity transition threshold.

### 1. Thermal Inversion Metric
$$\Delta T_{\text{inv}}(T_{\text{eq}}) = T_{\text{bright, 4.5}}(\tau=1) - T_{\text{bright, 3.6}}(\tau=1)$$
