## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Baxter et al. (2021) discover a sharp transition slope in Spitzer $3.6\,\mu$m and $4.5\,\mu$m secondary eclipse depths above $T_{\text{eq}} \sim 2200$ K due to $H^-$ opacity and thermal dissociation.

### 1. Secondary Eclipse Depth Scaling
$$D_{\text{ecl}}(\lambda) = \frac{B_\lambda(T_{\text{day}})}{B_\lambda(T_\star)} \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2$$
