## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Fisher & Heng (2018) present an analytical retrieval analysis of 38 hot Jupiter transmission spectra, establishing correlations between optical scattering index $\gamma$ and planetary equilibrium temperature $T_{\text{eq}}$.

### 1. Analytical Transmission Depth Formula
$$\Delta \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2 = \frac{2 R_p H}{R_\star^2} \left[ \gamma_{\text{Eul}} + \ln\left(\frac{\kappa_0 P_0}{g} \left(\frac{\lambda}{\lambda_0}\right)^{-\gamma}\right) \right]$$
