## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Naoz et al. (2011) demonstrate that octupole-order Eccentric Kozai-Lidov (EKL) resonance can flip planetary orbits from prograde to retrograde ($i > 90^\circ$), producing misaligned Hot Jupiters.

### 1. Octupole Order Parameter
$$\epsilon_{\text{oct}} = \frac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2} \frac{a_1}{a_2} \frac{e_2}{1 - e_2^2}$$

### 2. Retrograde Orbit Flip Condition
Orbit flips to $i > 90^\circ$ when $\epsilon_{\text{oct}} \gtrsim 0.001$ and $i_0 \sim 60^\circ - 120^\circ$.
