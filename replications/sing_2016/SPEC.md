## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Sing et al. (2016) perform a landmark HST/Spitzer survey of 10 hot Jupiters, establishing that water absorption attenuation is driven by cloud/haze opacity rather than chemical water depletion.

### 1. Cloud-Muffled Water Absorption
$$\Delta \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2_{1.4\mu\text{m}} = \frac{2 R_0 H}{R_\star^2} \ln \left(1 + \frac{P_0}{P_{\text{cloud}}}\right)$$
