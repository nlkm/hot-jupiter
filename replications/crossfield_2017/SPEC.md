## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Crossfield & Kreidberg (2017) compile HST transmission spectroscopy across sub-Jovian exoplanets ($1.5\,R_\oplus \le R_p \le 6.0\,R_\oplus$), identifying an empirical trend where water feature amplitude $A_{\text{H2O}}$ increases with equilibrium temperature $T_{\text{eq}}$ and planet radius $R_p$.

### 1. Water Absorption Amplitude Formula
$$A_{\text{H2O}} = \frac{\Delta(R_p/R_\star)_{1.4\,\mu\text{m}}}{H / R_\star}$$
