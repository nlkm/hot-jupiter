**Journal**: The Astrophysical Journal (ApJ), 847, 29 (2017) | **arXiv**: `1705.10810`

---

## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Owen & Wu (2017) derive the origin of the bimodal radius distribution of close-in sub-Neptunes and super-Earths driven by XUV photoevaporative mass loss.

### 1. Hydrodynamic Photoevaporative Mass Loss
$$\dot{M}_{\text{XUV}} = \eta \frac{\pi R_p^3 F_{\text{XUV}}}{G M_{\text{core}}}$$

### 2. Evaporative Radius Valley Scaling
$$R_{\text{valley}}(P_{\text{orb}}) = 1.8 \, R_\oplus \left(\frac{P_{\text{orb}}}{10\,\text{days}}\right)^{-0.15}$$
