## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Lothringer & Barman (2019) demonstrate how host star spectral type (F, G, K, M) alters NUV metal opacity (Fe I, Ti I) and thermal inversion strength in ultra-hot Jupiters.

### 1. Stellar Spectral Type & Thermal Inversion
$$T(P) = \left[ \frac{3 T_{\text{int}}^4}{4} \left(\tau + \frac{2}{3}\right) + \frac{3 T_{\text{eq}}^4}{4} \gamma_{\text{UV}} E_2(\tau_{\text{UV}}) \right]^{1/4}$$
