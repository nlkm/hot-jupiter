\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Benneke et al. (2019) (\textit{Nature Astronomy}, 3, 813) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Benneke2019K218bModel}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 = 1.0000$) across K2-18b HST WFC3 transmission spectra $(R_p/R_\star)^2(\lambda)$ and retrieved $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ volume mixing ratio posteriors.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Benneke et al. (2019) detect water vapor and cloud decks in habitable-zone sub-Neptune K2-18b:
\begin{equation}
\left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2(\lambda) = \left(\frac{R_0}{R_\star}\right)^2 + \frac{2 R_0 H}{R_\star^2} \left[\tau_0 + \ln \left(X_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} \sigma_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}(\lambda) + X_{\text{cloud}} \sigma_{\text{cloud}}\right)\right]
\end{equation}
