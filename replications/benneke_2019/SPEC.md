## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Benneke et al. (2019) present joint HST and Spitzer transmission spectroscopy of K2-18b, discovering liquid water droplets/cloud condensation in its atmosphere.

### 1. Cloud-Condensation Transmission Depth Formula
$$\Delta \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2 = \frac{2 R_p H}{R_\star^2} \ln\left(1 + \frac{P_0}{P_{\text{cloud}}} + \frac{\kappa_{\text{H2O}}(\lambda) X_{\text{H2O}} P_0}{\mu g}\right)$$
