## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Kreidberg et al. (2014) present high-precision HST WFC3 transmission spectroscopy of the super-Earth GJ 1214b, revealing a featureless spectrum that requires high-altitude clouds or hazes.

### 1. High-Altitude Cloud Suppression
$$\left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2(\lambda) = \left(\frac{R_0}{R_\star}\right)^2 + \frac{2 R_0 H}{R_\star^2} \ln \left[1 + \frac{P_0}{P_{\text{cloud}}}\right]$$
