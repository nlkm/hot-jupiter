## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Kreidberg et al. (2014) present HST WFC3 transmission observations of the super-Earth GJ 1214b, ruling out unclouded water-rich and solar metallicity atmospheres due to a featureless, flat spectrum constrained by high-altitude clouds.

### 1. Cloud Deck Transmission Spectrum Formula
$$\left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2(\lambda) = \left(\frac{R_{\text{cloud}}}{R_\star}\right)^2 = 1.345\%$$
