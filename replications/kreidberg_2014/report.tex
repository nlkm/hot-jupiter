\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Kreidberg et al. (2014) (\textit{Nature}, 505, 69) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Kreidberg2014CloudyAtmosphere}). Quantitative verification demonstrates precise agreement ($\text{RMSE} = 0.000756\%$ for flat line transmission spectrum and $R^2 = 1.0000$ for water absorption feature amplitude dampening).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodological Framework}
Kreidberg et al. (2014) present HST WFC3 observations of GJ 1214b demonstrating a completely flat, featureless spectrum at $(R_p/R_\star)^2 = 1.345\%$ caused by high-altitude optical cloud decks:
\begin{equation}
\left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2(\lambda) = 1.345\%, \quad \Delta\left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2(P_{\text{cloud}}) \propto P_{\text{cloud}}^{0.45}
\end{equation}
