## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Changeat et al. (2020) present HST WFC3 transmission spectroscopy and Bayesian retrieval of the inflated super-puff KELT-11b, constraining water abundance and cloud deck pressure.

### 1. Transmission Transit Depth & Scale Height
$$\Delta \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2 \approx \frac{2 R_p H}{R_\star^2} \ln\left(\frac{P_0}{P_{\text{ref}}}\right)$$
$$H = \frac{k_B T_{\text{term}}}{\mu g}$$
