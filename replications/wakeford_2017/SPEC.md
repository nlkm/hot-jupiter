## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Wakeford et al. (2017) measure the complete 0.2–5.0 $\mu$m transmission spectrum of the warm Neptune HAT-P-26b ($M_p = 0.059\,M_J$), deriving a low metallicity $[M/H] = 4.8 \times$ solar.

### 1. Mass-Metallicity Scaling Formula
$$\log_{10} [M/H] = \alpha \log_{10}(M_p / M_\oplus) + \beta$$
