\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Lothringer et al. (2018) (\textit{ApJ}, 866, 27) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Lothringer2018UltraHotJupiter}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 \ge 0.9944$) across ultra-hot Jupiter thermal inversion profiles $T(P)$ and emergent emission spectra $F_\lambda(\lambda)$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Lothringer et al. (2018) model the transition to ultra-hot Jupiters at high incident fluxes ($F_{\text{inc}} \ge 10^9\text{ erg s}^{-1}\text{ cm}^{-2}$):
\begin{equation}
T_{\text{eq}}(F_{\text{inc}}) = \left(\frac{F_{\text{inc}}}{4 \sigma_{\text{SB}}}\right)^{1/4}
\end{equation}
