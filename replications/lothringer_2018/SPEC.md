## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Lothringer et al. (2018) present radiative equilibrium models for ultra-hot Jupiters demonstrating how NUV opacity (Fe I, Mg I, $H^-$) creates severe upper-atmosphere thermal inversions.

### 1. NUV Opacity & Thermal Inversion
$$T(P) = \left[ \frac{3 T_{\text{int}}^4}{16} (2 + 3\tau) + \frac{3 T_{\text{eq}}^4}{16} \left( 2 + \gamma \left( 1 + (1 - \gamma\tau) e^{-\gamma\tau} \right) \right) \right]^{1/4}$$
