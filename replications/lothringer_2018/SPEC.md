## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Lothringer et al. (2018) model the atmospheric transition from hot to ultra-hot Jupiters, demonstrating how NUV/optical Fe I and Fe II opacities drive thermal inversions for $F_{\text{inc}} \ge 10^9\text{ erg s}^{-1}\text{ cm}^{-2}$.

### 1. Incident Stellar Flux Scaling Formula
$$T_{\text{eq}}(F_{\text{inc}}) = \left(\frac{F_{\text{inc}}}{4 \sigma_{\text{SB}}}\right)^{1/4}$$
