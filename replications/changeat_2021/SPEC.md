## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Changeat et al. (2021) demonstrate the detection of $HCN$ opacity in the transmission spectrum of HD 209458b from HST and Spitzer data.

### 1. Transmission Spectrum & Molecular Retrieval
$$\left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2(\lambda) = \left(\frac{R_0}{R_\star}\right)^2 + \frac{2 R_0 H}{R_\star^2} \left[\tau_0 + \ln\left(\sum_i X_i \sigma_i(\lambda) + X_{\text{HCN}} \sigma_{\text{HCN}}(\lambda)\right)\right]$$
