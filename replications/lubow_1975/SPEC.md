## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Lubow & Shu (1975) formulate the classical gas dynamics of semi-detached binary mass transfer through the L1 Lagrange point nozzle.

### 1. 1D Sonic L1 Nozzle Stream Density Equation
$$\dot{M}_{\text{L1}} = 2 \pi \rho_0 c_s \frac{c_s^2}{\Omega^2 \sqrt{|\Phi''_{xx} \Phi''_{yy}|}}$$

### 2. Stream Deflection Angle
$$\tan \theta_{\text{stream}} = -0.46 \left(\frac{M_p}{M_\star}\right)^{1/5}$$
