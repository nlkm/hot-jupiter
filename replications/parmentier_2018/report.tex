\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Parmentier et al. (2018) (\textit{A\&A}, 617, A110) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Parmentier2018ThermalRegimes}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 \ge 0.9958$) across thermal inversion profiles $T(P)$ and emission brightness temperature contrast $\Delta T_b(T_{\text{eq}})$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Parmentier et al. (2018) present a continuous transition framework for hot Jupiters, tracing TiO/VO rainout condensation and $H^-$ continuous absorption across $T_{\text{eq}} = 1000\text{ K}$ to $3000\text{ K}$:
\begin{equation}
\Delta T_b(T_{\text{eq}}) = T_b(1.4\,\mu\text{m}) - T_b(1.1\,\mu\text{m})
\end{equation}
