## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Parmentier et al. (2018) present a unified model for exoplanet atmospheres spanning 1000 K to 3000 K, tracking TiO/VO rainout condensation and $H^-$ opacity emergence.

### 1. Brightness Temperature Contrast Formula
$$\Delta T_b(T_{\text{eq}}) = T_b(1.4\,\mu\text{m}) - T_b(1.1\,\mu\text{m})$$
