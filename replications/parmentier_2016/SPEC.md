## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Parmentier et al. (2016) model cloud condensation boundaries and cloud composition transitions across hot Jupiter thermal regimes.

### 1. Vapor Pressure & Condensation Curve Equation
$$\ln P_{\text{vap}} = A - \frac{B}{T_{\text{cond}}}$$
for silicates ($MgSiO_3$) and manganese sulfide ($MnS$).
