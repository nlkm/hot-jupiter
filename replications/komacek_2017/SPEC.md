## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Komacek et al. (2017) compare 3D GCM thermal contrast predictions with Spitzer and Kepler phase curve observations across a population of 24 hot Jupiters.

### 1. Observational Phase Curve Contrast Formula
$$A_{\text{obs}} = \frac{F_{\text{day}} - F_{\text{night}}}{F_{\text{day}} + F_{\text{night}}}$$
