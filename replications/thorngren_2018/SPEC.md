## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Thorngren & Fortney (2018) quantify the thermal heating efficiency $\eta(T_{\text{eq}})$ required to explain hot Jupiter radius inflation as a function of incident flux and interior power deposition.

### 1. Heating Efficiency Gaussian Peak Model
$$\eta(T_{\text{eq}}) = \eta_{\text{max}} \exp \left[ -\frac{(T_{\text{eq}} - T_{\text{peak}})^2}{2 \sigma_T^2} \right]$$

where $\eta_{\text{max}} \approx 2.5\%$, $T_{\text{peak}} \approx 1500\,\text{K}$, $\sigma_T \approx 300\,\text{K}$.
