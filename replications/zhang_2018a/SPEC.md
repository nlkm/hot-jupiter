## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Zhang & Showman (2018a) present unified principles governing wave-mean flow interaction, wave-driven equatorial superrotation $U_{\text{eq}}$, and day-night flux contrast $\mathcal{A}_{\text{dn}}$ across tidally locked atmospheres.

### 1. Equatorial Superrotation Speed & Flux Contrast
$$U_{\text{eq}} \sim \sqrt{g H \frac{\Delta T_{\text{eq}}}{T_{\text{eq}}}} \cdot \left(\frac{\tau_{\text{rad}}}{\tau_{\text{wave}}}\right)^{1/2}$$
$$\mathcal{A}_{\text{dn}} = \frac{F_{\text{day}} - F_{\text{night}}}{F_{\text{day}} + F_{\text{night}}}$$
