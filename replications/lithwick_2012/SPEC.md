## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Lithwick & Wu (2012) analyze the Chirikov overlap of first-order mean-motion resonances driving dynamical chaos and orbital instability in tightly packed multi-planet systems.

### 1. Chirikov Resonance Overlap Criterion
$$\frac{\delta a}{a} = 1.3 \left(\frac{m_p}{M_\star}\right)^{2/7}$$

### 2. Secular Precession Frequency
$$g = \frac{1}{4} n \alpha b_{3/2}^{(1)}(\alpha) \left(\frac{m_p}{M_\star}\right)$$
