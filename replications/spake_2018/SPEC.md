## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Spake et al. (2018) discover metastable helium triplet $\text{He}(2^3S)$ absorption at $10833$ Å in WASP-107b's extended exosphere, directly observing atmospheric escape.

### 1. Metastable Helium Absorption
$$\Delta \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2_{1083\text{nm}} = \frac{2 R_p}{R_\star^2} \int n_{\text{He}^*}(s) \sigma_{1083}(s) \, ds$$
