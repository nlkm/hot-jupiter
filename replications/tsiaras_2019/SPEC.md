## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Tsiaras et al. (2019) detect water vapor ($H_2O$) in the HST WFC3 transmission spectrum of the habitable-zone super-Earth K2-18b ($M_p = 8.63 M_\oplus$), determining a hydrogen-rich envelope.

### 1. Transmission Scale Height Formula
$$\Delta \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2 = \frac{2 R_p H}{R_\star^2} \ln\left(\frac{\kappa_{\text{H2O}}(\lambda) X_{\text{H2O}} P_0}{\mu g}\right)$$
