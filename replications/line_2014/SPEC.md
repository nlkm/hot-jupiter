## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Line et al. (2014) establish a systematic Bayesian atmospheric retrieval framework for hot Jupiter emission spectra across Hubble and Spitzer wavelengths.

### 1. Analytic Double-Gray Radiative Equilibrium
$$T^4(P) = \frac{3 T_{\text{int}}^4}{4} \left[\tau + \frac{2}{3}\right] + \frac{3 T_{\text{eq}}^4}{4} \left[\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{\gamma \sqrt{3}} + \left(\gamma \sqrt{3} - \frac{1}{\gamma \sqrt{3}}\right) e^{-\gamma \tau \sqrt{3}}\right]$$
