## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Batygin & Morbidelli (2013) construct a canonical Hamiltonian perturbation theory for first-order mean-motion resonances (MMRs) in multi-planet systems.

### 1. Resonant Pendulum Hamiltonian
$$\mathcal{H} = \frac{1}{2} A \Phi^2 - B \sqrt{2 \Psi} \cos \sigma$$

### 2. Resonance Libration Width
$$\frac{\Delta a}{a} = 2 \sqrt{\frac{3 \mu}{a}} \left[ f_d(\alpha) e_1 + f_g(\alpha) e_2 \right]^{1/2}$$
