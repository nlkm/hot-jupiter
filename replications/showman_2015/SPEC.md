## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Showman et al. (2015) investigate 3D global circulation models of hot Jupiters, analyzing equatorial superrotation jets and Matsuno-Gill atmospheric wave dynamics.

### 1. Thermal Hotspot Phase Shift Formula
$$\Delta \phi_{\text{hotspot}} = \arctan\left(\frac{U_{\text{eq}} \tau_{\text{rad}}}{R_p}\right)$$
