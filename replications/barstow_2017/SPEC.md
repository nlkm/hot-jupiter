## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Barstow et al. (2017) present a systematic retrieval analysis of 10 HST/Spitzer hot Jupiters to constrain Rayleigh scattering slopes and cloud deck altitudes.

### 1. Transmission Spectrum Rayleigh Scattering Formula
$$\left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2(\lambda) = \left(\frac{R_{p,0}}{R_\star}\right)^2 + \frac{2 R_p}{R_\star^2} H \left[\ln\left(\frac{\sigma(\lambda)}{\sigma_0}\right) + \gamma \ln\left(\frac{\lambda}{\lambda_0}\right)\right]$$
