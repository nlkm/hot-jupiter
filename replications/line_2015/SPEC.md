**Journal**: The Astrophysical Journal (ApJ), 807, 183 (2015) | **arXiv**: `1505.06018`

---

## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Line et al. (2015) perform a uniform secondary eclipse retrieval on a sample of 19 hot Jupiters to derive the planetary mass-metallicity relation $[M/H]$ vs $M_p$.

### 1. Mass-Metallicity Scaling Formula
$$\log_{10}(Z / Z_\odot) = 0.5 - 1.0 \log_{10}(M_p / M_J)$$
