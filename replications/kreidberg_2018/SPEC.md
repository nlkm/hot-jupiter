## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Kreidberg et al. (2018) measure the phase-resolved thermal emission and longitudinal temperature structure of ultra-hot Jupiter WASP-103b using HST WFC3.

### 1. Phase Curve Flux Function
$$F(\phi) = C_0 + C_1 \cos(2\pi\phi - \phi_0) + C_2 \cos(4\pi\phi)$$
