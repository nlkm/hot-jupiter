## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Kreidberg et al. (2018) analyze HST and Spitzer phase curves of WASP-103b to determine day-to-night heat redistribution and thermal structure.

### 1. Phase Curve Thermal Emission Formula
$$\frac{F_p}{F_\star}(\phi) = F_0 + A_{\text{phase}} \cos(\phi - \Delta \phi)$$
