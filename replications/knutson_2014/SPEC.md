## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Knutson et al. (2014) present HST WFC3 transmission observations of HD 97658b, ruling out clear hydrogen-dominated atmospheres ($\mu = 2.3$) in favor of cloudy or high-metallicity atmospheres.

### 1. Transmission Spectrum Scale Height Formula
$$\left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2(\lambda) = 0.570\% + \frac{2 R_p}{R_\star^2} \frac{k_B T}{\mu g} \ln\left(\frac{\sigma(\lambda)}{\sigma_0}\right)$$
