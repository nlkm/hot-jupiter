## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Murray & Dermott (1999) formulate classical Laplace-Lagrange secular perturbation theory governing long-term eccentricity and inclination oscillations in multi-planet systems.

### 1. Laplace-Lagrange Secular Matrix
$$A_{ii} = \frac{1}{4} n_i \sum_{j \neq i} \frac{m_j}{M_\star} \alpha_{ij} b_{3/2}^{(1)}(\alpha_{ij}), \quad A_{ij} = -\frac{1}{4} n_i \frac{m_j}{M_\star} \alpha_{ij} b_{3/2}^{(2)}(\alpha_{ij})$$

### 2. Secular Eccentricity Evolution
$$e_1(t) = \sqrt{h_1(t)^2 + k_1(t)^2}$$
