## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Komacek & Showman (2016) derive scaling laws for day-night temperature differences in hot Jupiter atmospheres as a function of radiative and drag timescales.

### 1. Day-Night Temperature Contrast Scaling Law
$$\frac{\Delta T}{\Delta T_{\text{eq}}} = \frac{1}{1 + \tau_{\text{rad}} / \tau_{\text{wave}}}$$
where $\tau_{\text{wave}} = \frac{L}{c_s} \sqrt{1 + \frac{\tau_{\text{drag}}}{\tau_{\text{adv}}}}$.
