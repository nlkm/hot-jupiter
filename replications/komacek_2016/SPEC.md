## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Komacek & Showman (2016) derive analytical scaling laws for dayside-to-nightside thermal contrast $A$ driven by wave drag and radiative cooling.

### 1. Thermal Contrast Scaling Law
$$A = \frac{T_{\text{day}} - T_{\text{night}}}{T_{\text{day}} + T_{\text{night}}} = \frac{\tau_{\text{rad}} / \tau_{\text{wave}}}{1 + \tau_{\text{rad}} / \tau_{\text{wave}} + \gamma_{\text{drag}}}$$
