## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Fortney et al. (2007) provide structural models and radius grids for giant and rocky exoplanets as a function of mass, core mass, age, and orbital distance.

### 1. Planetary Radius Evolution Formula
$$R_p(M_p, M_{\text{core}}, t) = R_{\text{gas}}(M_p, t) \left[ 1 - C_{\text{core}} \left(\frac{M_{\text{core}}}{M_p}\right)^{0.6} \right]$$
