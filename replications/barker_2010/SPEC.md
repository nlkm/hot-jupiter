## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Barker & Ogilvie (2010) study the tidal evolution of hot Jupiters on inclined orbits, accounting for non-linear internal wave dissipation in stellar radiative cores.

### 1. Stellar Inclination Damping
$$\frac{\mathrm{d}i}{\mathrm{d}t} = -\frac{9}{4} \left(\frac{k_{2,\star}}{Q_\star'}\right) \left(\frac{M_p}{M_\star}\right) \left(\frac{R_\star}{a}\right)^5 n_{\text{orb}} \sin i (1 + \cos^2 i)$$
