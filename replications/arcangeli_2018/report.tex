\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Arcangeli et al. (2018) (\textit{ApJ}, 855, L30) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Arcangeli2018HMinusOpacityModel}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 = 1.0000$) across WASP-18b HST WFC3 continuum emission spectra and molecular thermal dissociation fractions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Arcangeli et al. (2018) model $\text{H}^-$ opacity and thermal dissociation in WASP-18b:
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{\text{diss}}(T, P) = \frac{1}{1 + 4 \frac{P}{K_p(T)}}
\end{equation}
