## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Arcangeli et al. (2018) observe the HST WFC3 secondary eclipse spectrum of the ultra-hot Jupiter WASP-18b ($T_{\text{eq}} > 2700\text{ K}$), revealing featureless $H^-$ continuum opacity caused by thermal dissociation of $H_2$.

### 1. Thermal Dissociation Fraction Formula
$$\alpha_H(P, T) = \frac{1}{1 + \sqrt{P / K_{\text{diss}}(T)}}$$
