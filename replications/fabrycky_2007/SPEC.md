## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Fabrycky & Tremaine (2007) model high-eccentricity migration of giant planets driven by Kozai-Lidov cycles coupled with equilibrium tidal friction (KCTF).

### 1. Tidal Pericenter Migration Cutoff
$$a_f \approx 2 a_0 (1 - e_{\text{max}})$$

### 2. Coupled Kozai-Tidal Rate
$$\frac{d e}{dt} = \left(\frac{de}{dt}\right)_{\text{Kozai}} + \left(\frac{de}{dt}\right)_{\text{tidal}}$$
