**Journal**: The Astrophysical Journal Letters (ApJL), 714, L238 (2010) | **arXiv**: `1002.3650`

---

## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Batygin & Stevenson (2010) demonstrate that atmospheric zonal winds cutting through stellar dipole magnetic fields induce electrical currents that deposit Ohmic power $P_{\text{Ohm}}$ deep in planetary interiors, inflating hot Jupiter radii.

### 1. Electrical Conductivity & Ohmic Dissipation
$$\sigma_{\text{elec}} = \frac{n_e e^2}{m_e \nu_{en}}$$

$$P_{\text{Ohm}} = \frac{1}{\sigma_{\text{elec}}} J^2 = \sigma_{\text{elec}} U^2 B^2$$
