## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Jia & Spruit (2018) investigate adiabatic and thermal envelope stripping of short-period gas giant planets overflowing their Roche lobes.

### 1. Polytropic Adiabatic Response Exponent
$$\zeta_{\text{ad}} = \left(\frac{d \ln R_p}{d \ln M_p}\right)_{\text{ad}} = \frac{1 - 2n}{3(1 + n)}$$
For convective gas giant envelopes ($n = 1.5$), $\zeta_{\text{ad}} = -4/15 \approx -0.267$.

### 2. Envelope Stripping Mass Loss Criterion
$$\dot{M}_{\text{env}} = \dot{M}_0 \left( \frac{M_{\text{env}}}{M_p} \right)^{1.5}$$
