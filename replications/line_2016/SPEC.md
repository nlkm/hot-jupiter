## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Line et al. (2016) perform an atmospheric retrieval of WASP-12b secondary eclipse spectra, deriving low water abundance constraints due to thermal inversion structure.

### 1. Emergent Emission Spectrum Formula
$$\frac{F_p}{F_\star}(\lambda) = \frac{B_\lambda(T(P))}{B_\lambda(T_\star)} \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2$$
