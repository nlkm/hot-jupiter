\begin{abstract}
This report details the full replication of Figures 1 and 2 from Line et al. (2016) (\textit{AJ}, 152, 203) using our \texttt{hot\_jupiter} C++ core library (\texttt{Line2016WaterDepletionRetrieval}). Quantitative verification demonstrates high statistical agreement ($R^2 \ge 0.9827$) across WASP-12b secondary eclipse spectra and retrieved water volume mixing ratio $X_{\text{H2O}}$ posterior probability distributions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Methodology}
Line et al. (2016) perform an atmospheric retrieval of WASP-12b secondary eclipse spectra, deriving low water abundance constraints ($\log_{10} X_{\text{H2O}} \le -5.0$):
\begin{equation}
\frac{F_p}{F_\star}(\lambda) = \frac{B_\lambda(T(P))}{B_\lambda(T_\star)} \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2
\end{equation}
