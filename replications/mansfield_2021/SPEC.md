## Executive Summary & Core Equations

Mansfield et al. (2021) confirm $1.4\,\mu$m $H_2O$ emission and a steep thermal inversion in the ultra-hot atmosphere of WASP-33b.

### 1. Emergent Emission Spectrum & Thermal Inversion
$$\frac{F_p}{F_\star}(\lambda) = \left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2 \frac{B_\lambda(T(P_{\text{em}}))}{B_\lambda(T_\star)}$$
