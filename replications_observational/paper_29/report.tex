\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Mysterious Interstellar Visitor}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
In October 2017, astronomers detected the very first known macroscopic object to visit our Solar System from another star: a reddish, cigar-shaped body named \textbf{1I/'Oumuamua} (Hawaiian for "scout" or "messenger from afar"). It entered our Solar System at a blistering speed of $26\,\text{km/s}$ on an unbound, hyperbolic orbit.

\subsection*{The Mystery: Why Did It Speed Up?}
As 'Oumuamua swung around the Sun and headed back into interstellar space, telescopes tracking its trajectory made an unexpected discovery: 'Oumuamua was traveling faster than gravity alone could explain! 
\begin{itemize}
    \item When a typical comet speeds up, it is because sunlight vaporizes subsurface ice into jets of gas and dust, acting like miniature rocket engines.
    \item But 'Oumuamua showed \textit{zero} visible dust or gas coma, prompting sensational theories about alien solar sails.
\end{itemize}
However, physics provides a natural answer: 'Oumuamua was likely an "iceberg" rich in pure molecular hydrogen ($\mathrm{H_2}$) or nitrogen ($\mathrm{N_2}$) ice. When vaporized by sunlight, these clean gases produce powerful rocket thrust without shedding any reflective dust.

\section*{Level 2: For the First-Year Undergrad — Hyperbolic Celestial Mechanics \& Rocket Thrust}

\subsection*{1. Hyperbolic Excess Velocity and Energy Conservation}
An interstellar body possesses positive specific orbital energy $\mathcal{E} > 0$ with eccentricity $e > 1$:
\begin{equation}
v^2(r) = v_\infty^2 + \frac{2 G M_\odot}{r}
\end{equation}
For 'Oumuamua ($v_\infty = 26.33\,\text{km/s}$, perihelion $q = 0.255\,\text{AU} = 3.81 \times 10^{10}\,\text{m}$), the perihelion velocity is:
\begin{equation}
v_{\text{peri}} = \sqrt{(26.33 \times 10^3\,\text{m/s})^2 + \frac{2 (6.674 \times 10^{-11})(1.989 \times 10^{30})}{3.81 \times 10^{10}\,\text{m}}} \approx 87.7\,\text{km/s}
\end{equation}

\subsection*{2. Non-Gravitational Rocket Equation}
Thrust produced by asymmetric sublimation of mass loss rate $\dot{M}$ with exhaust velocity $v_{\text{th}} \approx \sqrt{k_B T / \mu m_H}$ imparts a non-gravitational acceleration:
\begin{equation}
a_{\text{ng}} = \frac{F_{\text{thrust}}}{M} = \frac{\xi \dot{M} v_{\text{th}}}{M}
\end{equation}
where $\xi \approx 0.5$ is the directional collimation factor. For an object of mass $M \approx 4 \times 10^7\,\text{kg}$ and an observed acceleration $a_{\text{ng}}(1\,\text{AU}) = 4.92 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{m/s}^2$, the required outgassing rate is merely $\dot{M} \approx 1.5\,\text{kg/s}$—easily supplied by a small fraction of its solar-irradiated surface.

\subsection*{1. Modified Marsden Non-Gravitational Formulation}
We numerically integrate the equations of motion for 'Oumuamua incorporating the classical Marsden-Sekanina-Yeomans acceleration:
\begin{equation}
\ddot{\boldsymbol{r}} = -\frac{G M_\odot}{r^3} \boldsymbol{r} + \sum_{p} \boldsymbol{F}_{\text{planet},p} + A_1 g(r) \hat{\boldsymbol{r}} + A_2 g(r) \hat{\boldsymbol{T}} + A_3 g(r) \hat{\boldsymbol{N}}
\end{equation}
where the radial force parameter is $A_1 = (4.92 \pm 0.16) \times 10^{-6}\,\text{m/s}^2$ and $g(r) = (1\,\text{AU} / r)^2$. Astrometric orbital fitting across an 80-day baseline strictly constrains the transverse acceleration to $A_2 = (-0.02 \pm 0.08) \times 10^{-6}\,\text{m/s}^2$, consistent with isotropic day-side outgassing without substantial thermal lag.

\subsection*{2. Radiation Pressure vs. Hydrogen Ice Energetics}
Solar radiation pressure (SRP) acceleration is $a_{\text{SRP}} = (1 + A) L_\odot / (4\pi c r^2 \rho D) \approx 0.05 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{m/s}^2$, falling short of the observed acceleration by two orders of magnitude unless 'Oumuamua had an unphysically low bulk density $\rho < 0.01\,\text{g/cm}^3$ (a thin sheet). Conversely, $\mathrm{H_2}$ / $\mathrm{N_2}$ sublimation enthalpy ($\Delta H_{\mathrm{sub}} \approx 0.9 - 7.0\,\text{kJ/mol}$) matches the $1/r^2$ thermal scaling without producing visible dust grains.
