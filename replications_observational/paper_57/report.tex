\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Giant Cotton Candy Planet}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Located 200 light-years away in the constellation Virgo, \textbf{WASP-107b} is an extraordinary world. While it is nearly the same size as giant Jupiter ($0.94\,R_{\text{Jup}}$), it has only the mass of Neptune ($30.5\,M_\oplus$), making it one of the lowest-density, "puffiest" planets ever found—with a density like \textbf{cotton candy} ($0.13\,\text{g/cm}^3$)!

\subsection*{Why It Has Sulfur Clouds and Sand Rain in Its Sky}
Using the *James Webb Space Telescope*, astronomers discovered that WASP-107b has active photochemistry in its upper atmosphere, creating pungent sulfur dioxide ($\mathrm{SO_2}$) gas from stellar ultraviolet light. Even more astonishing, deep internal tidal heating continuously pumps energy into the planet, boiling clouds of microscopic quartz and silicate grains ($\mathrm{MgSiO_3}$) into high-altitude sand vapor that rains through the clouds!

\section*{Level 2: For the First-Year Undergrad — Scale Height Amplification \& Tidal Inflation}

\subsection*{1. Atmospheric Scale Height \& Transmission Amplitude}
Because WASP-107b has an exceptionally low surface gravity:
\begin{equation}
g_p = \frac{G M_p}{R_p^2} \approx \frac{(6.674 \times 10^{-11})(1.82 \times 10^{26})}{(6.72 \times 10^7)^2} \approx 2.69\,\text{m/s}^2 \quad (0.27\,g_\oplus)
\end{equation}
its atmospheric scale height $H$ is enormous:
\begin{equation}
H = \frac{k_B T_{\text{eq}}}{\mu_{\text{gas}} g_p} \approx \frac{(1.381 \times 10^{-23})(740\,\text{K})}{(2.3 \times 1.66 \times 10^{-27}\,\text{kg})(2.69\,\text{m/s}^2)} \approx 990\,\text{km}
\end{equation}
yielding huge transmission modulation signals $\Delta \delta \approx \frac{2 R_p (5 H)}{R_\star^2} \approx 600 - 800\,\text{ppm}$.

\subsection*{2. Tidal Heating \& Low Core Mass}
Standard thermal cooling models require an internal tidal dissipation heat flux of $F_{\text{int}} \approx 1.2\,\text{W/m}^2$ driven by ongoing eccentricity damping to maintain its bloated radius at an age of $3\,\text{Gyr}$.

\subsection*{1. $\mathrm{SO_2}$ Photochemical Column Production}
$\mathrm{SO_2}$ is generated via UV photolysis of hydrogen sulfide $\mathrm{H_2S} + h\nu \to \mathrm{HS} + \mathrm{H}$ followed by oxidation with $\mathrm{OH}$:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{SO} + \mathrm{OH} \to \mathrm{SO_2} + \mathrm{H}
\end{equation}
The observed $\mathrm{SO_2}$ mixing ratio $\chi(\mathrm{SO_2}) = (2.0 \pm 0.5) \times 10^{-5}$ requires an elevated atmospheric metallicity of $Z \approx 10 - 20\times\,\text{solar}$.
