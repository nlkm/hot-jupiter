\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — Six Planets Packed in the Orbit of Mercury}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Located 2,150 light-years away in the constellation Cygnus, \textbf{Kepler-11} stunned astronomers when NASA's *Kepler* space telescope discovered a family of \textbf{six low-density planets} orbiting so closely to their Sun-like star that the inner five planets would all fit comfortably inside the orbit of Mercury!

\subsection*{How Gravitational Tugs Weigh Alien Worlds}
Because these six worlds orbit in nearly perfect lockstep like a synchronized cosmic clock, they constantly tug on each other. When planet $d$ overtakes planet $c$, its gravity pulls planet $c$ forward, causing it to cross the star a few minutes early, and later pulls it backward, making it late. By measuring these tiny \textbf{Transit Timing Variations} (TTVs) down to the minute, astronomers weighed all six planets without ever seeing their gravitational wobble from Earth!

\section*{Level 2: For the First-Year Undergrad — Resonant Perturbations \& Super-Periods}

\subsection*{1. First-Order Mean-Motion Resonance Dynamics}
Planets $c$ ($P_c = 13.025\,\text{days}$) and $d$ ($P_d = 22.687\,\text{days}$) have period ratio $P_d / P_c \approx 1.7417$, lying near first-order resonances.
The resonant perturbation frequency is governed by the distance to exact integer resonance $\Delta = \frac{P_d}{P_c} - \frac{j}{j-1}$.
The resulting TTV super-period is:
\begin{equation}
P_{\text{super}} = \frac{1}{|j/P_d - k/P_c|} \approx 100.5\,\text{days}
\end{equation}

\subsection*{2. TTV Amplitude \& Planet Mass Scaling}
The sinusoidal timing perturbation amplitude $V_c$ scales directly with the perturbing planet's mass $m_d / M_\star$:
\begin{equation}
V_c \approx \frac{P_c}{2\pi} \frac{m_d}{M_\star} \frac{f_d(\alpha)}{\Delta} \approx 24.5\,\text{minutes}
\end{equation}
allowing precise mass determinations of $m_c = 2.9\,M_\oplus$ and $m_d = 7.3\,M_\oplus$.

\begin{equation}
\frac{d h_j}{dt} = \sum_{k=1}^6 A_{jk} k_k, \quad \frac{d p_j}{dt} = \sum_{k=1}^6 B_{jk} q_k
