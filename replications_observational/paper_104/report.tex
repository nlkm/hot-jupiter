\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Mirror World at the Edge of Darkness}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Cruising through the freezing depths of the outer solar system nearly three times further from the Sun than Pluto (96 AU), \textbf{(136199) Eris} is a massive dwarf planet that caused the historic reclassification of Pluto in 2006.

\subsection*{A Freshly Glazed Cosmic Mirror}
Eris has one of the brightest and most reflective surfaces of any body in the Solar System, reflecting \textbf{96\% of all incoming sunlight} like fresh snow! In its distant, 557-year eccentric orbit, when Eris travels far from the Sun, its entire atmosphere of nitrogen and methane freezes completely solid, falling to the ground as a pristine, reflective layer of frozen frost that perpetually resurfaces the world!

\section*{Level 2: For the Undergrad — Volatile Frost Sublimation \& Hapke Scattering}

\subsection*{1. Thermal Equilibrium \& Vapor Pressure Freeze-Out}
At aphelion ($r \approx 96\,\text{AU}$), solar insolation is diluted to $F_{\odot}(r) = L_\odot / (4\pi r^2) \approx 0.15\,\text{W/m}^2$. The surface equilibrium temperature with geometric albedo $p_V = 0.96$:
\begin{equation}
T_{\text{surf}} = \left(\frac{(1 - A) F_{\odot}}{\epsilon \sigma_{\text{SB}}}\right)^{1/4} \approx 30.0\,\text{K}
\end{equation}
At $30\,\text{K}$, the saturation vapor pressure of nitrogen ($P_{\text{vap}}(\text{N}_2) \sim 10^{-4}\,\text{Pa}$) and methane ($P_{\text{vap}}(\text{CH}_4) \sim 10^{-10}\,\text{Pa}$) drops by orders of magnitude, causing complete atmospheric collapse onto the surface.

\subsection*{2. Hapke Radiative Transfer \& Methane Band Shifts}
Near-infrared reflectance spectra exhibit pure $\mathrm{CH}_4$ vibrational overtone absorption bands at $1.66, 1.72, 2.20,$ and $2.32\,\mu\text{m}$. A subtle blue-shift ($\Delta\lambda \approx -1.5\,\text{nm}$) indicates that methane ice is diluted within a dominant solid nitrogen ($\mathrm{N}_2$) crystalline matrix.
