\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Ice Volcano of the Asteroid Belt}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Rising abruptly out of the smooth cratered plains of dwarf planet \textbf{Ceres}, \textbf{Ahuna Mons} is a solitary, massive mountain \textbf{2.5 miles tall} ($4\,\text{km}$) and $12\,\text{miles wide}$ ($20\,\text{km}$)—half the height of Mount Everest!

\subsection*{Volcanoes That Erupt Salt and Ice}
Unlike volcanoes on Earth that spew glowing molten rock, Ahuna Mons is a \textbf{cryovolcano} (ice volcano). Deep underground, pockets of salty water and mud built up pressure and slowly squeezed up to the surface like toothpaste, freezing into a steep, glistening dome decorated with bright white streaks of sodium carbonate salt!

\section*{Level 2: For the First-Year Undergrad — Bingham Plastic Dome Mechanics}

\subsection*{1. Ceres Micro-Gravity \& Hydrostatic Magma Pressure}
For Ceres ($R = 470\,\text{km}, M = 9.38 \times 10^{20}\,\text{kg}$), the surface gravity is $g = 0.28\,\text{m/s}^2$. The hydrostatic driving pressure head $\Delta P$ from a subsurface reservoir at depth $d = 30\,\text{km}$ with density contrast $\Delta\rho = 150\,\text{kg/m}^3$ is:
\begin{equation}
\Delta P = \Delta\rho g d \approx 150 \times 0.28 \times 30,000 \approx 1.26 \times 10^6\,\text{Pa} \quad (12.6\,\text{bar})
\end{equation}
Providing sufficient overpressure to lift dense cryomagma to the surface.

\subsection*{2. Bingham Slurry Yield Stress \& Dome Geometry}
A non-Newtonian Bingham fluid resists flow until shear stress exceeds a critical yield strength $\tau_y$. For an axisymmetric extruded dome of height $H$ and basal radius $R_{\text{base}}$, the static aspect ratio is:
\begin{equation}
\tau_y \approx \frac{\rho_{\text{slurry}} g H^2}{2 R_{\text{base}}}
\end{equation}
For $\rho_{\text{slurry}} \approx 1300\,\text{kg/m}^3, H = 4000\,\text{m}, R_{\text{base}} = 10,000\,\text{m}$, this derives an effective yield stress:
\begin{equation}
\tau_y \approx \frac{1300 \times 0.28 \times 1.6 \times 10^7}{2 \times 10^4} \approx 1.46 \times 10^4\,\text{Pa} \quad (14.6\,\text{kPa})
\end{equation}
Matching laboratory rheological measurements of cold, viscous water-ice-silicate-brine slurries.

\subsection*{1. Conductive Crustal Pocket Solidification}
The lifetime of the liquid cryomagma source pocket of radius $L \approx 10\,\text{km}$ under Stefan conductive cooling is:
\begin{equation}
\tau_{\text{freeze}} \approx \frac{L^2}{\kappa} \left( 1 + \frac{L_{\text{fusion}}}{c_p \Delta T} \right) \approx 200 - 500\,\text{Myr}
\end{equation}
Where $\kappa \approx 1.0 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$ is the crustal thermal diffusivity, confirming Ahuna Mons formed geologically recently ($\tau_{\text{crater}} < 250\,\text{Myr}$).
