\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Bursting Frozen Moon}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Pluto's largest companion, the icy moon \textbf{Charon}, is split right across its equator by a colossal canyon system known as \textbf{Serenity Chasma}. Spanning over 1,000 miles in length and plunging up to \textbf{5 miles (8 kilometers) deep}, these canyons make the Grand Canyon look like a tiny scratch!

\subsection*{How a Freezing Subsurface Ocean Cracked the Moon's Crust}
When water turns into ice, it expands by about \textbf{9\%}. In Charon's ancient youth, it held a warm liquid water ocean under its ice crust. As radioactive heat decayed, the subterranean ocean slowly froze solid. The immense expanding ice exerted overwhelming pressure from within, literally stretching and cracking Charon's rigid surface until titanic canyon rifts ruptured across the entire moon!

\section*{Level 2: For the First-Year Undergrad — Volumetric Phase Expansion \& Lithospheric Strain}

\subsection*{1. Phase Transition Volume Expansion}
The liquid water to Ice-Ih transition features density decrease $\rho_{\text{liquid}} \approx 1000\,\text{kg/m}^3 \to \rho_{\text{ice}} \approx 917\,\text{kg/m}^3$, yielding volumetric expansion $\frac{\Delta V}{V_{\text{ocean}}} \approx 0.09$.
For an ocean comprising mantle volume fraction $f_{\text{ocean}} \approx 0.30$, global radial expansion is:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\Delta R}{R_{\text{Charon}}} \approx \frac{1}{3} f_{\text{ocean}} \left( \frac{\Delta V}{V} \right) \approx 0.007 - 0.020 \quad (0.7\% - 2.0\%)
\end{equation}
resulting in a planetary radius increase $\Delta R \approx 5 - 12\,\text{km}$.

\subsection*{2. Tensile Hoop Stresses \& Brittle Graben Faulting}
Under thin spherical shell elasticity, global expansion generates tensile hoop stress:
\begin{equation}
\sigma_\theta = \frac{E}{1 - \nu} \left( \frac{\Delta R}{R_0} \right) \approx \frac{10\,\text{GPa}}{1 - 0.30} (0.02) \approx 280\,\text{MPa}
\end{equation}
far exceeding the macroscopic tensile strength of cryogenic water ice ($\sigma_{\text{crit}} \approx 25\,\text{MPa}$), triggering normal fault graben systems with cumulative vertical throws $h_{\text{scarp}} \approx 8.0\,\text{km}$.

\begin{equation}
\rho C_p \frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = \frac{1}{r^2} \frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left( k r^2 \frac{\partial T}{\partial r} \right) + H_{\text{radio}}(t)
\end{equation}
locking the Serenity Chasma graben network into permanent fossilized cryotectonic topography.
