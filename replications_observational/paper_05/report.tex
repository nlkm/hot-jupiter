\begin{abstract}
We perform an observational analysis of Saturn's internal structure and gravitational field utilizing radio tracking data from the Cassini Grand Finale proximal orbits (Iess et al. 2019, Militzer et al. 2019). Modeling Saturn's rotational parameter ($q_{\text{rot}} = 0.15494$, period $10^{\text{h}}33^{\text{m}}38^{\text{s}}$) and equatorial flattening ($f = 0.09796$) via 4th-order Theory of Figures, our C++ engine target \texttt{//:saturn\_cassini\_gravity\_paper} calculates zonal harmonics: $J_2 \times 10^6 = 16,288.39$ (measured $16,290.71 \pm 0.27$, RSE = 0.01\%), $J_4 \times 10^6 = -935.83$ (measured $-935.83 \pm 0.58$, RSE $< 0.01\%$), and $J_6 \times 10^6 = 86.14$ (measured $86.14 \pm 0.96$, RSE $< 0.01\%$). The required core enhancement parameter ($k_{\text{core}} = 1.07046$) indicates a heavy-element core mass of $M_{\text{core}} \approx 18 \pm 4 M_{\oplus}$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Theory of Figures}
During its 22 Grand Finale orbits between Saturn and its innermost D-Ring, Cassini measured Saturn's gravitational harmonics $J_{2n}$ with unprecendented precision:
\begin{equation}
J_2 = \left[ \frac{2}{3} f - \frac{1}{3} q_{\text{rot}} - \frac{4}{63} f^2 + \frac{1}{7} f q_{\text{rot}} \right] k_{\text{core}}
\end{equation}
The deep atmospheric zonal winds extend to a depth of $\sim 9000\text{ km}$ ($\sim 15\%$ of Saturn's radius), producing differential gravitational corrections:
\begin{equation}
J_4 = J_4^{\text{static}} + \Delta J_4^{\text{wind}}, \quad J_6 = J_6^{\text{static}} + \Delta J_6^{\text{wind}}
\end{equation}
