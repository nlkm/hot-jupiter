\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Wobble that Proved Einstein Right}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
For over half a century, astronomers were baffled by an unsolved cosmic riddle: the closest point of \textbf{Mercury}'s egg-shaped orbit around the Sun was slowly pivoting faster than Isaac Newton's laws of gravity could explain by \textbf{43 arcseconds per century} (a tiny angle equal to the width of a dime seen from two miles away).

\subsection*{Curved Spacetime and the Spacecraft Orbiting the Sun}
In 1915, Albert Einstein solved the mystery: the Sun's immense mass warps the fabric of space and time. As Mercury whips through this gravitational gravity well at $100{,}000\,\text{mph}$, the curvature of space nudges its orbit forward. In modern times, NASA's *MESSENGER* spacecraft tracked Mercury's position to within a few feet using radar and radio waves, confirming Einstein's prediction with breathtaking perfection!

\section*{Level 2: For the First-Year Undergrad — General Relativistic Orbit Precession}

\subsection*{1. Schwarzschild Geodesic Precession Derivation}
In the Schwarzschild metric around the Sun ($M_\odot = 1.989 \times 10^{30}\,\text{kg}$), the orbital radial equation of motion for Mercury ($a = 0.3871\,\text{AU}$, $e = 0.2056$) with $u = 1/r$ is:
\begin{equation}
\frac{d^2 u}{d\phi^2} + u = \frac{G M_\odot}{h^2} + \frac{3 G M_\odot}{c^2} u^2
\end{equation}
Perturbative analysis of the relativistic $\frac{3GM}{c^2}u^2$ term yields an angular perihelion shift per orbital revolution:
\begin{equation}
\Delta\varpi_{\text{rev}} = \frac{6\pi G M_\odot}{c^2 a (1 - e^2)} \approx 5.019 \times 10^{-7}\,\text{radians/orbit} \quad (0.1035''/\text{orbit})
\end{equation}

\subsection*{2. Secular Century Precession Rate}
With orbital period $P = 87.969\,\text{days}$ ($N = 415.20\,\text{orbits/century}$):
\begin{equation}
\dot{\varpi}_{\text{GR}} = N \Delta\varpi_{\text{rev}} \approx 42.98''/\text{century}
\end{equation}
Adding Newtonian perturbations from other planets ($531.63''/\text{cy}$) and the solar quadrupole moment $J_2 \approx 2.25 \times 10^{-7}$ ($0.03''/\text{cy}$) yields total observed precession $\dot{\varpi}_{\text{total}} = 574.64''/\text{century}$.
