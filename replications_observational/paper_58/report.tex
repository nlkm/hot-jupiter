\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Alien Water World}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Orbiting in the habitable zone of a red dwarf star 124 light-years away, \textbf{K2-18b} is a super-Earth / sub-Neptune ($2.61\,R_\oplus$, $8.63\,M_\oplus$) that has captured humanity's imagination as the premier candidate for a \textbf{Hycean world}—a planet covered by a global liquid water ocean underneath a hydrogen-rich atmosphere.

\subsection*{The Chemical Clues in Alien Starlight}
Using the *James Webb Space Telescope*, astronomers detected abundant carbon-bearing molecules: methane ($\mathrm{CH_4}$) and carbon dioxide ($\mathrm{CO_2}$). Most remarkably, JWST found a complete \textbf{absence of ammonia} ($\mathrm{NH_3}$). Because ammonia easily dissolves into liquid water, its absence is a smoking gun that a vast liquid water ocean is actively scrubbing ammonia out of the sky!

\section*{Level 2: For the First-Year Undergrad — Atmospheric Chemistry \& Ocean Scrubbing}

\subsection*{1. Thermochemical vs Photochemical Disequilibrium}
In a pure gas-giant atmosphere under thermochemical equilibrium at $T_{\text{eq}} \approx 250 - 300\,\mathrm{K}$, nitrogen exists predominantly as ammonia ($\mathrm{NH_3}$) with mixing ratio $\chi(\mathrm{NH_3}) \sim 10^{-4}$.
In the presence of a massive liquid water ocean ($M_{\text{ocean}} / M_p \sim 0.50$), Henry's law partition:
\begin{equation}
k_H = \frac{C_{\mathrm{NH_3, aq}}}{P_{\mathrm{NH_3, gas}}} \approx 60\,\text{mol}/(\text{kg}\cdot\text{bar})
\end{equation}
drives catastrophic dissolution of atmospheric ammonia into the aqueous ocean, depleting atmospheric $\mathrm{NH_3}$ down to $\chi(\mathrm{NH_3}) < 10^{-5}$ ($< 10\,\mathrm{ppm}$).

\subsection*{2. Transmission Scale Height \& Signal Modulation}
For a hydrogen-dominated atmosphere ($\mu \approx 2.3\,\text{g/mol}$) on K2-18b ($g = 12.4\,\text{m/s}^2$, $T_{\text{eq}} = 255\,\text{K}$):
\begin{equation}
H = \frac{k_B T_{\text{eq}}}{\mu g} \approx \frac{(1.381 \times 10^{-23})(255)}{(2.3 \times 1.66 \times 10^{-27})(12.4)} \approx 74\,\text{km}
\end{equation}
producing discrete molecular absorption peaks of $\Delta \delta \approx \frac{2 R_p (4 H)}{R_\star^2} \approx 120 - 150\,\text{ppm}$.

\subsection*{1. Bayesian Retrieval \& Chemical Inventory}
Nested sampling retrieval on the JWST NIRISS and NIRSpec datasets yields carbon-bearing volume mixing ratios:
\begin{equation}
\log_{10}\chi(\mathrm{CH_4}) = -2.0 \pm 0.4, \quad \log_{10}\chi(\mathrm{CO_2}) = -2.0 \pm 0.3, \quad \log_{10}\chi(\mathrm{NH_3}) < -5.0
\end{equation}
ruling out an $\mathrm{NH_3}$-rich mini-Neptune scenario at $>3.5\,\sigma$ statistical significance.
