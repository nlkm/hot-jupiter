\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Moon That Popped Its Seams}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Pluto's giant moon \textbf{Charon} is split by a monumental system of chasms and canyons named \textbf{Serenity Chasma}. This cosmic canyon is \textbf{5 miles deep} ($8\,\text{km}$) and over $600\,\text{miles long}$—four times deeper than the Grand Canyon!

\subsection*{Why Freezing Water Tears Moons Apart}
Billions of years ago, Charon held a warm liquid water ocean beneath its icy shell. As the moon cooled, the ocean froze. Because liquid water expands by $7\%$ when turning into ice, the freezing ocean expanded from within like a soda bottle in the freezer, bursting Charon's brittle crust and creating gigantic rift valleys!

\section*{Level 2: For the First-Year Undergrad — Cryospheric Volumetric Strain \& Fracture}

\subsection*{1. Phase Transition Volumetric Strain}
The phase transformation of liquid water ($\rho_w \approx 1000\,\text{kg/m}^3$) to Ice I ($\rho_i \approx 920\,\text{kg/m}^3$) produces a fractional volume expansion:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\Delta V}{V} = \frac{\rho_w - \rho_i}{\rho_i} \approx \frac{80}{920} \approx +8.7\% \implies \Delta V_{\text{eff}} \approx +7.0\%
\end{equation}
For a global subsurface ocean shell of thickness $d_{\text{ocean}} \approx 100\,\text{km}$ in Charon ($R = 606\,\text{km}$), the radial expansion is $\Delta R \approx \frac{1}{3} \frac{\Delta V}{V} \left( \frac{R_{\text{ocean}}}{R} \right)^3 R \approx 12 - 15\,\text{km}$.

\subsection*{2. Tensile Membrane Stress in Thin Elastic Shell}
For an elastic lithosphere of thickness $h \approx 40\,\text{km}$ and Young's modulus $E \approx 9 \times 10^9\,\text{Pa}$, the isotropic tensile membrane stress $\sigma_\theta$ is:
\begin{equation}
\sigma_\theta = \frac{E}{1 - \nu} \frac{\Delta R}{R} \approx \frac{9 \times 10^9}{0.7} \times \frac{14}{606} \approx 2.97 \times 10^8\,\text{Pa} \quad (300\,\text{MPa})
\end{equation}
Greatly exceeding the tensile fracture strength of polycrystalline water ice ($\sigma_{\text{crit}} \approx 25\,\text{MPa}$), driving global normal fault graben rifting.
