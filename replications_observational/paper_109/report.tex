\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Planet Covered in a Global Ocean}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Located 124 light-years away in the constellation Leo, \textbf{K2-18b} is a super-Earth / mini-Neptune (2.6 times the size of Earth, 8.6 times Earth's mass) orbiting squarely inside the habitable zone of its red dwarf star where liquid water can exist.

\subsection*{A Hydrogen Sky Over a Water World}
In late 2023, the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) made an astonishing discovery in K2-18b's atmosphere. JWST detected abundant **methane ($\mathrm{CH}_4$) and carbon dioxide ($\mathrm{CO}_2$)**, but found a near-total absence of ammonia ($\mathrm{NH}_3$). In chemistry, ammonia dissolves rapidly into liquid water. The missing ammonia, combined with abundant carbon molecules and a light hydrogen envelope, strongly suggests that K2-18b is a **Hycean world**—a planet wrapped in a hydrogen-rich atmosphere covering a planet-wide, global liquid water ocean!

\section*{Level 2: For the Undergrad — Atmospheric Chemistry \& Hycean Equilibrium}

\subsection*{1. Thermochemical Equilibrium \& Ammonia Depletion}
In a solar-composition hydrogen atmosphere at equilibrium temperature $T_{\text{eq}} \approx 250 - 300\,\text{K}$, nitrogen should predominantly reside in ammonia ($\mathrm{NH}_3$). However, in the presence of a vast liquid ocean interface:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{NH}_3(\text{gas}) + \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}(\text{liq}) \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{NH}_4^+ + \mathrm{OH}^-
\end{equation}
Henry's law ocean solubility strips gas-phase ammonia below the photochemical detection limit ($\text{VMR}(\mathrm{NH}_3) < 10\,\text{ppm}$), while methane ($\mathrm{CH}_4 \sim 1\%$) and carbon dioxide ($\mathrm{CO}_2 \sim 1\%$) remain gaseous.

\subsection*{2. Scale Height \& Transmission Feature Amplitude}
The low mean molecular weight ($\mu \approx 2.3 - 2.8\,\text{amu}$) of the hydrogen-rich envelope generates a puffy scale height $H = k_B T / \mu m_{\text{amu}} g \approx 120\,\text{km}$, yielding prominent $\sim 200 - 300\,\text{ppm}$ transit absorption features across $0.8 - 5.2\,\mu\text{m}$.

\subsection*{1. Bulk Interior Structure \& Water Fraction}
Equation-of-state modeling reveals K2-18b comprises an Earth-like silicate-iron core ($m_{\text{core}} \approx 4.0\,M_\oplus$), a high-pressure supercritical/liquid water mantle ($m_{\text{ocean}} \approx 4.5\,M_\oplus$, $\sim 50\%$ by mass), and a thin outer $\mathrm{H}_2/\mathrm{He}$ gaseous envelope ($m_{\text{env}} \approx 0.05\,M_\oplus, \sim 0.6\%$).
