\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Ghostly Wheels on Saturn's Rings}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
When NASA's Voyager spacecraft flew past \textbf{Saturn} in 1980, they spotted eerie, radial dark fingers and ghostly streaks flickering across the brilliant icy B Ring like the spokes of a turning bicycle wheel.

\subsection*{How Static Electricity Lifts Floating Dust Clouds}
These mysterious spokes are clouds of tiny, microscopic ice dust grains (smaller than a single bacterium). When microscopic meteoroids crash into Saturn's rings or when ring particles interact with sunlight, they create clouds of electrically charged plasma. The ring grains charge up with static electricity ($-15\,\text{Volts}$) until they repel the ring plane and \textbf{levitate 50 miles (80 km) above the rings}! At first, the spokes rotate rigidly locked to Saturn's magnetic field ($10.66\,\text{hours}$), before orbital gravity shears them into swept-back streaks over several hours!

\section*{Level 2: For the Undergrad — Dusty Plasma Physics \& Magnetic Corotation}

\subsection*{1. Electrostatic Grain Levitation Equilibrium}
A spherical sub-micron water ice dust grain of radius $a_{\text{grain}} \approx 0.60\,\mu\text{m}$ immersed in the ring photoelectron sheath acquires an equilibrium surface potential $\Phi \approx -15\,\text{V}$. The total net charge is:
\begin{equation}
q = 4\pi \epsilon_0 a_{\text{grain}} \Phi \approx -1.0 \times 10^{-15}\,\text{C} \quad (\sim 6{,}000\,\text{electrons})
\end{equation}
The vertical electrostatic repelling force $F_E = q E_z$ overcomes the net vertical gravitational restoring force of Saturn $F_g = m_g \Omega_K^2 z$, accelerating the grain to a maximum levitation height:
\begin{equation}
z_{\text{lev}} \approx \frac{q E_0}{m_g \Omega_K^2} \approx 80\,\text{km}
\end{equation}

\subsection*{2. Magnetic Corotation \& Differential Keplerian Shear}
During the rapid formation phase ($\tau_{\text{form}} \sim 5 - 10\,\text{min}$), radial plasma currents lock the spoke orientation to Saturn's magnetic dipole rotation ($\Omega_{\text{mag}} = 2\pi / 10.656\,\text{hr}$). Once decoupled, differential Keplerian shear tears the spoke at rate:
\begin{equation}
\frac{d\theta}{dt} = \Omega_K(r_{\text{inner}}) - \Omega_K(r_{\text{outer}}) \approx 18.5^\circ/\text{hr}
\end{equation}

\subsection*{1. Forward Scattering Mie Inversion \& Seasonal Variation}
High-phase-angle optical imaging ($\alpha > 140^\circ$) proves the spokes are forward-scattering Mie scatterers dominated by narrow size distributions ($a_{\text{eff}} = 0.60 \pm 0.10\,\mu\text{m}$). Spoke activity peaks at Saturnian equinox when low solar ring opening angles reduce the dayside Debye shielding.
