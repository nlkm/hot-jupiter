\subsection*{1. Microgravity Ballistic Kinematics}
On Miranda's airless surface ($R_M = 235.8\,\text{km}$, $M_M = 6.4 \times 10^{19}\,\text{kg}$), the surface gravitational acceleration is:
\begin{equation}
g_M = \frac{G M_M}{R_M^2} \approx 0.079\,\text{m/s}^2 \quad (0.008\,g_\oplus)
\end{equation}
From a cliff of vertical relief $h_0 = 20.0\,\text{km}$, the 1D collisionless descent equations are:
\begin{equation}
z(t) = h_0 - \frac{1}{2} g_M t^2, \quad v(t) = g_M t
\end{equation}
The total freefall duration to reach the base $z(t_{\text{fall}}) = 0$ is:
\begin{equation}
t_{\text{fall}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 h_0}{g_M}} = \sqrt{\frac{4.0 \times 10^4\,\text{m}}{0.079\,\text{m/s}^2}} \approx 712\,\text{seconds} \quad (11.87\,\text{minutes})
\end{equation}
with terminal impact velocity $v_{\text{impact}} = g_M t_{\text{fall}} \approx 56.2\,\text{m/s}$ ($202.4\,\text{km/h}$).
