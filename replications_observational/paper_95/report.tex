\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Moon That Burst Its Own Skin}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Pluto's largest companion, \textbf{Charon}, is an icy world half the size of Pluto. Running across its entire equator is one of the most gigantic canyon systems in the solar system: \textbf{Serenity Chasma}, a chasm \textbf{5 miles (8 km) deep and over 600 miles long} (more than four times deeper than the Grand Canyon!).

\subsection*{What Caused This Planetary-Scale Fracture?}
Billions of years ago, Charon possessed a warm, liquid water subsurface ocean beneath its outer ice shell. As Charon cooled down over geological time, the ocean froze into solid ice. Because water uniquely expands by \textbf{7\% to 9\% when it freezes}, the freezing ocean pushed outwards against the cold crust like ice expanding inside a sealed glass bottle, building up immense pressure until Charon literally cracked open along its equator, tearing apart into massive canyons!

\section*{Level 2: For the Undergrad — Volumetric Phase Changes \& Thin-Shell Fracture Mechanics}

\subsection*{1. Volumetric Expansion of Ice-Ih}
The phase transformation of liquid water ($\rho_w \approx 1000\,\text{kg/m}^3$) to Ice-Ih ($\rho_i \approx 920\,\text{kg/m}^3$) generates a fractional volume expansion:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\Delta V}{V} = \frac{\rho_w - \rho_i}{\rho_i} \approx +0.087 \quad (+8.7\%)
\end{equation}
In a spherical body of radius $R_C = 606\,\text{km}$ with an initial ocean thickness $d_{\text{ocean}} \approx 100\,\text{km}$, freezing increases the moon's radius by $\Delta R \approx 4 - 6\,\text{km}$, producing global isotropic extensional strain:
\begin{equation}
\epsilon = \frac{\Delta R}{R_C} \approx 0.01 - 0.02 \quad (1 - 2\%)
\end{equation}

\subsection*{2. Lithospheric Tensile Stress \& Graben Inversion}
In an elastic ice shell of Young's modulus $E \approx 9\,\text{GPa}$ and Poisson's ratio $\nu \approx 0.33$, the membrane tensile stress is:
\begin{equation}
\sigma_t = \frac{E}{1 - \nu} \epsilon \approx 30 - 40\,\text{MPa}
\end{equation}
which vastly exceeds the brittle tensile strength of ice ($\sigma_{\text{crit}} \approx 25\,\text{MPa}$), inducing extensional normal faulting and forming graben troughs of depth $h = 8\,\text{km}$.
