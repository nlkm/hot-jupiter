\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Smoggy Mystery World}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Located 48 light-years away in the constellation Ophiuchus, \textbf{GJ 1214b} is the archetypal ``sub-Neptune'' exoplanet—a category of planets bigger than Earth but smaller than Neptune that are completely missing from our own Solar System.

\subsection*{A Giant Blank Wall of Photochemical Smog}
For over a decade, astronomers pointed every major space telescope at GJ 1214b hoping to detect water, methane, or steam in its atmosphere. Instead, they saw a completely featureless, flat transmission spectrum! Finally, the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) solved the enigma: GJ 1214b is wrapped in a thick, impenetrable blanket of **organic photochemical hydrocarbon haze** (soot and tholins), combined with an atmosphere enriched \textbf{500 times heavier in metals} than our Sun, which compresses its scale height and blocks all starlight from probing below its high-altitude smog layer!

\section*{Level 2: For the Undergrad — Atmospheric Scale Height \& Aerosol Extinction}

\subsection*{1. High-Metallicity Mean Molecular Weight Compression}
The atmospheric scale height is governed by mean molecular weight $\mu$:
\begin{equation}
H = \frac{k_B T_{\text{eq}}}{\mu m_{\text{amu}} g_p}
\end{equation}
For GJ 1214b ($M_p = 8.17\,M_\oplus, R_p = 2.74\,R_\oplus, g_p = 10.7\,\text{m/s}^2, T_{\text{eq}} \approx 550\,\text{K}$), a hydrogen-dominated atmosphere ($\mu \approx 2.3\,\text{amu}$) produces $H \approx 180\,\text{km}$ ($\Delta\delta \sim 1000\,\text{ppm}$). However, high-metallicity enrichment ($[\text{M/H}] \sim 500\times$, $\mu \approx 12 - 18\,\text{amu}$) compresses the scale height down to:
\begin{equation}
H \approx 25 - 35\,\text{km}
\end{equation}

\subsection*{2. Photochemical Aerosol Extinction \& Flatlining}
Small organic tholin haze particles ($a_{\text{haze}} \approx 0.05\,\mu\text{m}$) produced by stellar UV methane photolysis generate optical extinction cross-sections $\sigma_{\text{ext}} \propto \lambda^{-p}$ ($p \approx 0.8 - 1.2$), creating an optically thick surface at millibar levels that truncates molecular absorption lines across $1.0 - 12.0\,\mu\text{m}$.

\subsection*{1. Thermal Phase Curve Inversion \& Reflected Light}
JWST MIRI $5 - 12\,\mu\text{m}$ full-orbit phase curves measure dayside $T_{\text{day}} = 553 \pm 12\,\text{K}$ and nightside $T_{\text{night}} = 437 \pm 19\,\text{K}$, confirming moderate heat circulation ($A_B = 0.51 \pm 0.06$) and ruling out pure metal-free primordial envelopes at $>10\sigma$.
