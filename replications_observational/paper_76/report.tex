\section*{Level 1: For the Non-Scientist — The Celestial Rubber Duck Powered by Jet Engines}

\subsection*{The Big Picture}
Comet \textbf{67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko} is a bizarre, two-lobed icy wanderer that resembles a giant celestial rubber duck spanning 2.5 miles across, formed by the gentle ancient merger of two icy planetesimals.

\subsection*{How Jet Engines of Sublimating Ice Alter a Comet's Path}
As Comet 67P swoops close to the Sun near its perihelion ($1.24\,\text{AU}$), intense solar heat boils underground water and carbon dioxide ice into vapor. Colossal supersonic jets blast out into vacuum, shooting over \textbf{300 gallons (1 ton) of water per second} into space! Because the jets fire unevenly from the comet's neck (the Hapi region), this reaction acts like natural rocket thrusters, steadily altering the comet's orbit and rotation period.

\section*{Level 2: For the Undergrad — Marsden Non-Gravitational Rocket Forces}


\subsection*{1. Marsden Sublimation Law $g(r_h)$}
The standard empirical sublimation rate function for water ice $g(r_h)$ is given by:
\begin{equation}
g(r_h) = \alpha \left(\frac{r_h}{r_0}\right)^{-m} \left[ 1 + \left(\frac{r_h}{r_0}\right)^n \right]^{-k}
\end{equation}
with normalization $g(1\,\text{AU}) = 1$, $r_0 = 2.808\,\text{AU}$, $m = 2.15$, $n = 5.09$, and $k = 4.614$.

\subsection*{2. Non-Gravitational Acceleration Vector}
The rocket recoil acceleration vector $\mathbf{a}_{\text{ng}}$ is decomposed in the orbital frame $(\hat{\mathbf{r}}, \hat{\mathbf{t}}, \hat{\mathbf{n}})$:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{a}_{\text{ng}} = g(r_h) \left( A_1 \hat{\mathbf{r}} + A_2 \hat{\mathbf{t}} + A_3 \hat{\mathbf{n}} \right)
\end{equation}
For Comet 67P, orbital solutions yield:
\begin{equation}
A_1 \approx 3.25 \times 10^{-8}\,\text{AU/day}^2, \quad A_2 \approx 0.82 \times 10^{-8}\,\text{AU/day}^2
\end{equation}
producing an orbital period change $\Delta P \approx -0.36\,\text{days}$ per perihelion revolution.

\subsection*{1. Asymmetric Outgassing \& Seasonal Thermal Inertia Lag}
In situ mass spectrometry by Rosetta ROSINA reveals peak production $Q_{\text{max}} = (3.5 \pm 0.5) \times 10^{28}\,\text{molecules/s}$ occurs $20\,\text{days}$ post-perihelion due to the high thermal inertia of southern hemisphere dust mantles:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{F}_{\text{rocket}} = -\int_{\mathcal{S}} \rho_{\text{gas}}(\mathbf{r}) (\mathbf{v}_{\text{gas}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{n}}) \mathbf{v}_{\text{gas}} d\mathcal{S}
\end{equation}
inducing a secular spin-down of the nucleus rotation period by $\Delta P_{\text{rot}} \approx -21\,\text{minutes}$.
