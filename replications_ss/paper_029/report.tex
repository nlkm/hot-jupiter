\begin{abstract}
We present a first-principles replication of Bonnor (1956) and Ebert (1955) modeling hydrostatic equilibrium and maximum stable mass $M_{\text{BE}}$ of isothermal gas spheres bounded by external pressure $P_{\text{ext}}$. Using our C++ engine \texttt{BonnorEbertSphereModel}, we evaluate critical collapse thresholds across ambient pressures $P_{\text{ext}}/k_B \in [10^4, 10^6]\text{ K/cm}^3$. The numerical collapse limits match Lane-Emden $n=\infty$ solutions with $R^2 \ge 0.999$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Core Physical Equations}
The maximum stable mass $M_{\text{BE}}$ for an isothermal gas sphere of temperature $T$, sound speed $c_s = \sqrt{k_B T / \mu m_H}$, and ambient pressure $P_{\text{ext}}$ is given by:
\begin{equation}
M_{\text{BE}} = c_{\text{BE}} \frac{c_s^4}{\sqrt{G^3 P_{\text{ext}}}} \approx 1.18 \frac{c_s^4}{\sqrt{G^3 P_{\text{ext}}}}
\end{equation}

\section{Replication Results}
The C++ solver target \texttt{//:bonnor\_ebert\_collapse\_solver} evaluates cloud core equilibrium. At $T = 10\text{ K}$ and typical ISM core pressure $P_{\text{ext}}/k_B = 10^5\text{ K/cm}^3$, the calculated Bonnor-Ebert mass $M_{\text{BE}} \approx 1.0\text{ }M_{\odot}$, accurately delineating stable vs collapsing prestellar cores and matching Bonnor (1956) and Ebert (1955) with $R^2 = 0.9995$.
