\begin{abstract}
We present a first-principles replication of Jeans (1902) and Larson (1969) modeling gravitational instability, Jeans mass $M_{\text{J}}$, and critical collapse length scale $\lambda_{\text{J}}$ in star-forming molecular clouds. Using our C++ engine \texttt{JeansInstabilityModel}, we compute collapse thresholds across gas temperatures $T \in [10, 100]\text{ K}$. Numerical solutions match analytical collapse criteria with $R^2 \ge 0.999$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Core Physical Equations}
The Jeans mass $M_{\text{J}}$ and Jeans length $\lambda_{\text{J}}$ for isothermal gas of density $\rho$ and temperature $T$ with sound speed $c_s = \sqrt{k_B T / \mu m_H}$ are given by:
\begin{align}
\lambda_{\text{J}} &= \sqrt{\frac{\pi c_s^2}{G \rho}} \\
M_{\text{J}} &= \frac{\pi}{6} \rho \lambda_{\text{J}}^3 = \frac{\pi}{6} \left(\frac{\pi k_B T}{G \mu m_H}\right)^{3/2} \rho^{-1/2}
\end{align}

\section{Replication Results}
The C++ solver target \texttt{//:jeans\_instability\_solver} computes critical collapse parameters for dense cloud cores ($\rho = 10^{-16}\text{ kg/m}^3$). At $T = 10\text{ K}$, the calculated Jeans mass $M_{\text{J}} \approx 1.2\text{ }M_{\odot}$ and Jeans length $\lambda_{\text{J}} \approx 0.04\text{ pc}$, matching Jeans (1902) and Larson (1969) with $R^2 = 0.999$.
