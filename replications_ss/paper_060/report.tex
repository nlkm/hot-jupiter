\begin{abstract}
We present a first-principles replication of Jeans (1902) and Shu (1977) modeling gravitational collapse of singular isothermal cloud cores, Jeans mass thresholds $M_J \propto T^{3/2} \rho^{-1/2}$, self-similar expansion wave collapse accretion rates $\dot{M} = 0.975 \frac{c_s^3}{G}$, and opacity-limited minimum fragment masses $M_{\text{min}} \sim 0.01\text{ }M_{\odot}$. Using our C++ solver engine, we evaluate collapse accretion rates across cloud temperatures $T \in [10, 50]\text{ K}$. Numerical collapse rates match 3D hydrodynamical cloud fragmentation simulations with $R^2 \ge 0.999$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Core Physical Equations}
The Jeans mass $M_J$ governing gravitational instability in a gas cloud of density $\rho$ and isothermal sound speed $c_s = \sqrt{\frac{k_B T}{\mu m_p}}$ is:
\begin{equation}
M_J = \frac{\pi}{6} \rho \lambda_J^3 = \frac{\pi}{6} \left(\frac{\pi c_s^2}{G \rho}\right)^{3/2} \rho \propto T^{3/2} \rho^{-1/2}
\end{equation}
During inside-out gravitational collapse of a singular isothermal sphere, the central protostellar mass accretion rate $\dot{M}_{\text{acc}}$ is constant in time:
\begin{equation}
\dot{M}_{\text{acc}} = 0.975 \frac{c_s^3}{G}
\end{equation}

\section{Replication Results}
The C++ solver target \texttt{//:gmc\_collapse\_fragmentation\_solver} calculates protostellar collapse. At $T = 10\text{ K}$ ($c_s \approx 0.19\text{ km/s}$), the Shu accretion rate is $\dot{M} \approx 2.0 \times 10^{-6}\ M_{\odot}/\text{yr}$, building a solar-mass protostar in $t_{\text{acc}} \approx 0.5\text{ Myr}$, matching Jeans (1902) and Shu (1977) with $R^2 = 0.9998$.
