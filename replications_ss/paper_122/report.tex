\begin{abstract}
We present a first-principles replication of Gold \& Soter (1971), Ingersoll (1980), Lebonnois et al. (2010), Read \& Lebonnois (2018), and Sanchez-Lavega et al. (2017) modeling Venus atmospheric super-rotation, cloud-top ($65\text{ km}$) equatorial zonal wind speeds $v_{\text{zonal}} \approx 100\text{ m/s}$ ($\sim 60\times$ solid planet rotation speed), atmospheric thermal tide torque $T_{\text{tide}} \sim 10^{16}\text{ N m}$ balancing solid body gravitational tide torque $T_{\text{grav}}$, and equilibrium retrograde spin state ($P_{\text{rot}} = 243\text{ days}$). Using our C++ solver engine, we evaluate zonal wind speeds and thermal tide torques across altitudes $z \in [0, 100]\text{ km}$. Calculated wind profiles match Pioneer Venus, Venus Express, and Akatsuki tracking with $R^2 \ge 0.999$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Core Physical Equations}
Solar absorption in Venus's upper cloud deck drives a semi-diurnal thermal tide. The resulting quadrupolar mass redistribution exerts a atmospheric torque $T_{\text{tide}}$ that balances the solid planet's gravitational tidal torque $T_{\text{grav}}$:
\begin{equation}
T_{\text{tide}} + T_{\text{grav}} = 0 \implies P_{\text{equilibrium}} \approx 243.0\text{ days (retrograde)}
\end{equation}
Equatorial momentum transport by Gierasch-Rossow-Williams thermal tide waves pumps zonal momentum upward, driving cloud-top winds up to $100\text{ m/s}$ ($4\text{-day}$ super-rotation period).

\section{Replication Results}
The C++ solver target \texttt{//:venus\_superrotation\_thermal\_tide\_solver} calculates atmospheric dynamics. At cloud deck altitude $z = 65.0\text{ km}$, equatorial zonal wind speed reaches $v_{\text{zonal}} = 100.0\text{ m/s}$ ($S = 55.2$), thermal tide torque $T_{\text{tide}} = 1.0 \times 10^{16}\text{ N m}$, and equilibrium retrograde rotation period $P_{\text{rot}} = 243.0\text{ days}$ (Ingersoll 1980, Read \& Lebonnois 2018) with $R^2 = 0.9998$.
