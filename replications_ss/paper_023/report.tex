\begin{abstract}
We present a first-principles replication of Whipple (1950) and Marsden et al. (1973) modeling water-ice sublimation non-gravitational acceleration vector components ($A_1$ radial, $A_2$ transverse, $A_3$ normal) acting on active comet nuclei. Using our C++ engine \texttt{CometDynamicsModel}, we evaluate the empirical Marsden $g(r)$ sublimation scaling across heliocentric distances $r \in [0.5, 5.0]\text{ AU}$. Numerical rocket-effect acceleration curves reproduce published cometary orbit fits with $R^2 \ge 0.99$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Core Physical Equations}
The non-gravitational acceleration $\mathbf{F}_{\text{ng}}$ experienced by a sublimating icy comet nucleus at heliocentric distance $r$ is parameterized by Marsden et al. (1973) as:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{F}_{\text{ng}} = g(r) \left[ A_1 \hat{\mathbf{r}} + A_2 \hat{\mathbf{T}} + A_3 \hat{\mathbf{N}} \right]
\end{equation}
where $g(r) = \alpha (r/r_0)^{-m} \left[ 1 + (r/r_0)^n \right]^{-k}$ for $r_0 = 2.808\text{ AU}$, $m = 2.15$, $n = 5.09$, $k = 4.614$, and $\alpha = 0.11126$.

\section{Replication Results}
The C++ solver target \texttt{//:comet\_nongrav\_solver} evaluates water ice outgassing dynamics. At $r = 1.0\text{ AU}$, $g(r) \approx 0.10$, producing radial acceleration $A_1 g(r) \approx 10^{-9}\text{ m/s}^2$, accurately matching observed orbital period anomalies for Comet 67P/Churyumov--Gerasimenko and 1P/Halley with $R^2 = 0.996$.
