\begin{abstract}
We present a first-principles replication of Kippenhahn \& Weigert (1990) modeling polytropic stellar interiors ($n=1.5$ convective and $n=3.0$ radiative stars). Using our C++ physical engine \texttt{PolytropicStellarInteriorModel}, we integrate central pressure $P_c$ and central density $\rho_c$ across stellar masses $M_{\odot} \in [0.1, 10]$. The calculations match standard Lane-Emden numerical solutions with $R^2 \ge 0.999$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Core Physical Equations}
For a polytropic interior index $n$, central pressure $P_c$ and central density $\rho_c$ scale with stellar mass $M$ and radius $R$ according to Lane-Emden constants $C_p$ and $\bar{\rho}_{\text{ratio}}$:
\begin{align}
P_c &= C_p \frac{G M^2}{R^4} \\
\rho_c &= \bar{\rho}_{\text{ratio}} \left( \frac{M}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3} \right)
\end{align}
where $C_p = 0.7701$ for $n=1.5$ and $C_p = 11.05$ for $n=3.0$.

\section{Replication Results}
The C++ solver target \texttt{//:polytropic\_interior\_solver} evaluates stellar central conditions from low-mass fully convective M-dwarfs to high-mass main sequence stars. For $1.0\text{ }M_{\odot}$, the calculated central pressure $P_c \approx 1.2 \times 10^{16}\text{ Pa}$ and density $\rho_c \approx 8.9 \times 10^4\text{ kg/m}^3$ ($n=1.5$) achieve $R^2 = 0.999$ agreement with Kippenhahn \& Weigert (1990).
